# Case Study: Enterprise Cloud Cost Optimization

## Company Profile
- **Industry**: Financial Services
- **Cloud Provider**: AWS (Multi-region)
- **Initial Monthly Spend**: $500,000
- **Timeline**: 6-month optimization initiative

## Challenge
- Rapid cloud spending growth (30% YoY)
- Limited cost visibility across 50+ teams
- No cost allocation or accountability
- Minimal use of commitment-based discounts

## Strategy Implementation

### Phase 1: Inform (Months 1-2)
**Actions:**
- Implemented comprehensive tagging strategy (85% coverage achieved)
- Created cost dashboards for executives, managers, and engineers
- Established FinOps team with cross-functional members

**Results:**
- Full visibility into costs by team, environment, and service
- Identified top 10 cost drivers (80% of total spend)

### Phase 2: Optimize (Months 3-4)
**Actions:**
- Right-sized 200+ EC2 instances (average 30% reduction)
- Purchased Reserved Instances for stable workloads (60% coverage)
- Implemented auto-scaling for dev/test environments
- Cleaned up 50TB of orphaned EBS volumes and snapshots

**Results:**
- $80,000/month savings from right-sizing
- $120,000/month savings from Reserved Instances
- $15,000/month savings from storage cleanup

### Phase 3: Operate (Months 5-6)
**Actions:**
- Deployed automated policies for resource lifecycle
- Set up budget alerts for all teams
- Established monthly FinOps reviews
- Created self-service optimization tools for engineers

**Results:**
- Automated identification of optimization opportunities
- 95% of teams within budget
- Cultural shift toward cost awareness

## Final Results

### Cost Savings
- **Total Monthly Savings**: $215,000 (43% reduction)
- **Annual Savings**: $2.58M
- **ROI**: 15:1 (investment in tools and team)

### Operational Improvements
- Cost allocation accuracy: 95%
- RI/Savings Plan coverage: 75%
- Average resource utilization: +40%
- Time to provision with cost awareness: -20%

## Key Learnings

1. **Executive sponsorship is critical**: C-level buy-in accelerated adoption
2. **Start with visibility**: Can't optimize what you can't see
3. **Empower engineers**: Give teams tools and data to self-optimize
4. **Automation scales**: Manual processes don't scale beyond pilot
5. **Culture matters most**: Technical solutions need cultural change

## Recommendations
- Begin with quick wins to build momentum
- Invest in automation early
- Make cost data accessible and actionable
- Celebrate successes to reinforce behavior
